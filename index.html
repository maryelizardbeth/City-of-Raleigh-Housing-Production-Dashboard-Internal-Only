Data notes

AMI affordability breakdowns show only for Complete development projects (per HCD rules). The Homeowner Rehab, Homebuyer Assistance, and Other Housing Impact layers are public de-identified versions — resident names and addresses are removed and each point is shifted 200–700 m, so mapped locations are approximate. Mapped point counts are geometry and do not reconcile to the Housing Production Tracker totals (the source of truth) — see the charts below.
Prototype (V2) — template for staff use, not an approved public release. Housing layers served from the Raleigh AGOL org; reference layers from maps.raleighnc.gov. Brand: City of Raleigh. Design review: DesignTeam@raleighnc.gov.
